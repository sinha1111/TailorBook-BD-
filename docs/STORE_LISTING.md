# Store listing drafts

## English
**Title:** TailorBook BD - Tailor Orders
**Short (80):** Offline orders, measurements, payments and receipts for tailors.
**Full:** TailorBook BD replaces your paper measurement notebook and order register. Works fully offline - no account, no internet needed.
- Customers with phone, address and notes
- Measurements for Panjabi, shirt, pants, kurta, pajama, blouse and custom garments, in inches or cm, with version history
- Orders with several garments, delivery dates, discounts and status tracking; each order keeps its own measurement copy
- Advance and partial payments, due balances, payment corrections that keep history
- PDF receipts you can share or print, in Bengali or English
- Delivery reminders on your phone
- Reports: payments received, order value, dues, garment counts, CSV export
- Backup and restore your data to a file you keep
Your data stays on your phone. Make backups regularly - uninstalling the app or losing the phone without a backup loses your data.

- একাধিক পোশাকের অর্ডার, ডেলিভারির তারিখ, ছাড় ও অবস্থা ট্র্যাকিং; প্রতিটি অর্ডারে মাপের আলাদা কপি থাকে

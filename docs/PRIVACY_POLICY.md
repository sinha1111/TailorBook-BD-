# Privacy Policy - TailorBook BD (DRAFT - review with a qualified person before publishing)

Last updated: [date]. Developer: [name / company]. Contact: [support email].

**What the app stores.** Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, measurements, orders, payments and your shop details. This data is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

**What we collect.** Nothing. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK in this version. We do not receive, sell or share your data.

**Sharing you control.** Data leaves the device only when you choose to export or share a backup file, receipt PDF, measurement sheet or CSV. Where it goes then depends on the app you pick (e.g. Google Drive, WhatsApp) and is governed by that service.

**Notifications.** Delivery reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They use no server.

**Security.** The local database is not encrypted. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app. Use your phone's screen lock.

**Backup and deletion.** If you uninstall the app or lose your phone without a backup, your data is lost. You can delete all data in Settings -> Delete all data, or by uninstalling.

**Children.** The app is a business tool and is not directed to children.

**Changes.** If ads or purchases are added in future versions, this policy and the Play Data safety form will be updated first.
